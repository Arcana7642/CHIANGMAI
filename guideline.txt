[100-Point Eco-Driving Score]

Design a 100-point score for each delivery.

Initial weighting proposal:

- Total: 100 points

Because engine status cannot be determined from GPS speed alone, treat stopped time as an informational proxy or provide an alternative model that excludes it.

Recognize rapid-acceleration and rapid-deceleration events only when the threshold persists for at least 1.5 seconds.

N_10km = N × 10 / D

Define speeding as time spent at least 5 km/h over the road-specific speed limit.

[Conversion from Score to Fuel Savings]

For the initial MVP, linearly connect the score to the research-based eco-driving and non-eco-driving fuel economies.

Example values:

- Non-eco-driving fuel economy: 33.95 km/L
- Eco-driving fuel economy: 50.44 km/L
- Score 0: Non-eco-driving level
- Score 100: Eco-driving level

Baseline fuel consumption:

F_base = D / FE_non_eco

Fuel consumption at a score of 100:

F_eco = D / FE_eco

Estimated fuel consumption at score S:

F_estimated =
F_base -
(S / 100) × (F_base - F_eco)

Estimated fuel saved:

ΔF =
(S / 100) × D ×
(1 / FE_non_eco - 1 / FE_eco)

Estimated fuel-saving percentage:

R_F =
(1 - FE_non_eco / FE_eco) × (S / 100)

Clearly state that this linear mapping is an MVP assumption. Explain how it should later be replaced by a model calibrated using actual fuel-flow, refuelling, or controlled validation data.

[Fuel-Cost Calculations]

If the fuel price is P in THB/L:

Fuel cost = Fuel consumption × P

Fuel-cost savings:

ΔC = ΔF × P
